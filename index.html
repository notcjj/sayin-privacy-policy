Sayin Discord Application

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sayin (“the Bot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, store, and disclose information when it is added to or used in a Discord server.

1. Information We May Process

To provide server management, moderation, and automated moderation features, Sayin may process:

  • Discord user IDs and server (guild) IDs.
  • Usernames, display names, and relevant server or role information made available through Discord.
  • Moderation records, such as warnings, ban or timeout actions, reasons entered by moderators, and action timestamps.
  • Message content or message-related information when needed to evaluate automated moderation rules or carry out a moderation/logging feature configured by a server.
  • Bot command inputs and technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the Bot.

The exact information processed depends on which Sayin features are enabled and how the server is configured. We do not ask users for Discord passwords or authentication credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate the Bot, apply server moderation settings, detect or respond to rule violations, maintain moderation histories, provide moderation logs, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and improve reliability and security.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the Bot’s database or operational logs where required for its features. We intend to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required to address security, operational, or legal needs. Actual retention depends on the Bot’s implementation and server configuration.

Do not assume that removing the Bot from a server automatically deletes previously stored records. Contact the developer to ask about access or deletion of information associated with a server or user.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of Discord’s platform and may be handled by hosting, database, or other service providers used to operate Sayin. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce this policy, or protect users, servers, and the Bot.

Discord’s own privacy practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

5. Server Administrators and Moderation

Server administrators are responsible for configuring moderation features, choosing where moderation logs are sent, and ensuring their use of the Bot complies with applicable rules and laws. Moderation actions are generally initiated by server settings, moderators, or automated rules.

6. Data Requests and Deletion

For questions or requests concerning information processed by Sayin—including requests to access or delete eligible records—contact the developer using the contact details below. Please include the relevant Discord user ID and/or server ID so the request can be understood. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where legally or operationally necessary.

7. Children's Privacy

Sayin is a general-purpose Discord server utility and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must comply with Discord’s age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sayin’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the Bot after an update means the updated policy applies to subsequent use.
