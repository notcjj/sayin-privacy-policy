# Sayin Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 13, 2026

Welcome to Sayin! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Discord server and bot.

### 1. Information We Collect
- Discord User IDs and usernames.
- Server IDs and channel IDs.
- Messages and commands used with our bot, when necessary.
- Moderation records, such as warnings, mutes, and bans.

### 2. How We Use Information
- To provide and improve our Discord bot services.
- To maintain server safety and prevent abuse.
- To enforce server rules and moderation.
- To troubleshoot errors and improve functionality.

### 3. Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

### 4. Data Storage & Security
We take reasonable steps to protect collected information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

### 5. Data Removal
Users may contact the Sayin administration team to request the removal of eligible stored information.

### 6. Third-Party Services
Sayin operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have questions, concerns, or data removal requests, please contact the Sayin administration team through our Discord server.

**By using Sayin, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.**
